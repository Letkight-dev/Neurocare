Understanding Autism & Support

What is Autism?

Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) is a developmental condition characterized by challenges with social interaction, communication, sensory sensitivities, and restricted or repetitive behaviors. Because autism is a spectrum, it affects every individual differently, bringing both unique challenges and strengths.

Autism in Cambodia

In Cambodia, many children with autism are misdiagnosed due to a lack of specialized clinical centers and low community awareness. The lack of special educational infrastructure in local provinces means many children are left out of formal schooling. We work to train local public school teachers, supply sensory rooms, and aid parents' associations to support their children's growth.
