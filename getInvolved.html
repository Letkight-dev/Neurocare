Leadership

Three people steer NeuroCare’s programs, classrooms, and community partnerships.

CS

Chan Sophea

Executive Director

Sophea founded NeuroCare after years of supporting families seeking a diagnosis in Phnom Penh. She sets strategy and keeps programs close to parent needs.

MR

Meas Rithy

Director of Education

Rithy designs teacher workshops and sensory classroom kits. He previously taught in a public primary school and still coaches educators in the provinces.

SV

Sokha Vann

Community Partnerships Lead

Sokha builds volunteer teams and parent associations. She coordinates with clinics, special schools, and local authorities so families are not left to search alone.
