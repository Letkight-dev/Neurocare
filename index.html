Understanding Autism

Get answers to fundamental questions about autism to help dismantle stigma and create an inclusive environment.

What is Autism?
Autism, or Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD), is a neurological difference that affects how people communicate, interact, behave, and learn. It is not an illness, but a distinct way of experiencing the world.
What is the current situation of autism in Cambodia?
In Cambodia, public awareness about autism remains low, and families face difficulties in finding clinical diagnosis and specialized educators. Support groups like Parents Association of Autism are working to expand resources, training, and early screening tools.
How can I support neurodivergent individuals?
Support starts with empathy and patience. Accept and appreciate their unique sensory preferences, create sensory-friendly environments, communicate clearly, and advocate for inclusive education and workplace access.
