What We Do

Practical programs that help children, families, and teachers across Cambodia.

Our Programs

We focus on awareness, classrooms, and family support so more children can learn and belong.

Awareness Campaigns

Community talks, radio spots, and school visits that explain autism clearly and reduce stigma.

Teacher Training

Short courses for public school teachers on communication, sensory needs, and inclusive lesson planning.

Sensory Classrooms

We help schools set up quiet corners, lighting, and learning kits so students can stay calm and focused.

Family Support

Parent groups share daily strategies, connect to clinics, and advocate for school placement.

Early Screening

We partner with local clinics to train staff on developmental checklists and referral pathways.

Provincial Reach

Programs start in Phnom Penh and extend to Siem Reap, Battambang, and partner schools in the provinces.

How a gift is used

Donations go to classroom materials, teacher stipends for training days, and parent workshops. We keep administration lean so most funds reach families and schools.

Help a classroom this month

Your gift funds kits, lighting, and coaching for teachers who want to include every child.
